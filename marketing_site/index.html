BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few useful answers.

What exactly is ServoERP?

ServoERP is Windows business management software for Indian service SMEs. It brings customer records, contracts, jobs, billing, payments, purchasing, inventory and workforce administration into one application.

Is it a browser app or a desktop application?

It is a Windows desktop application backed by SQL Server. This website explains the product and lets you request a demo; it is not the ERP application itself.

Can we work when internet access is unreliable?

Core local office workflows can run using the local database. Connected features such as online messaging or delivery integrations require internet access and configuration.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter AMC covers up to 3 PCs, Growth AMC up to 7 PCs and Business AMC up to 15 PCs. Share your office setup during the demo to discuss the right fit. Annual prices are listed above.

Can you review our current workflow first?

Yes. Tell us how you handle contracts, service visits, stock and invoicing. The demo can focus on those workflows so you can evaluate the fit before deciding.

How do we move our existing information?

Data preparation and import requirements depend on your current records. Discuss your spreadsheets and required history during the rollout conversation before making a migration plan.

About the Creator

Hi, I’m Harshal Sonawane — Creator of ServoERP

I’m an IT professional with a strong interest in technology, automation, systems engineering, and solving real-world business problems.

ServoERP started with a simple observation: many businesses still depend on a mix of spreadsheets, disconnected applications, manual processes, and repetitive administrative work to manage their day-to-day operations. As a business grows, those small inefficiencies quickly become bigger problems.

I wanted to build something different.

My vision for ServoERP is to create a practical, reliable and continuously evolving business platform that brings the essential parts of an organisation together — customers, contracts, quotations, invoicing, payments, purchasing, inventory, employees, payroll, jobs, service operations and reporting — within one connected system.

But ServoERP is not just about putting features into one application.

The bigger goal is to reduce repetitive work, prevent human error, improve visibility and give businesses better control over their operations.

Wherever a process can be made safer, faster or smarter through good software and automation, I want ServoERP to eventually address it.

I have approached ServoERP not simply as a software project, but as a system that should evolve alongside the businesses using it.

Real operational problems influence what gets built, improved and automated next.

This is why ServoERP continues to grow.

Why I Built ServoERP

I believe powerful business software shouldn’t have to feel unnecessarily complicated.

Technology should quietly handle complexity in the background so that people can spend less time managing software and more time running their business.

One connected system. Less manual work. Fewer mistakes. Better decisions.

ServoERP is an ongoing journey, and my ambition is to keep developing it into an increasingly intelligent and dependable platform for managing modern business operations.
